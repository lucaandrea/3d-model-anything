Your home, in 3D

Start with your first room.

This space is clear and ready for your home. Capture a room on your iPhone, then bring the finished scan to this Mac.

  1. 01

    Scan on iPhone

    Open 3D Model Anything, create a house, and scan one room. Tap Finish room when you’re done.

  2. 02

    AirDrop to this Mac

    In the saved room, tap Share room (.zip) and choose AirDrop. Keep the original ZIP as your capture backup.

  3. 03

    Build your model

    Tell Codex where the ZIP was saved and ask it to use scan-to-threejs. Your model can then open in this viewer.

The current iPhone app shares completed scans. Live streaming to Safari is not available in this version.
