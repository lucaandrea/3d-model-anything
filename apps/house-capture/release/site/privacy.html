Privacy policy

3D MODEL ANYTHING · UPDATED OCTOBER 27, 2026

Your scans stay under your control

3D Model Anything does not require an account and does not send scans, photos, room layouts, or usage analytics to the developer. The app has no advertising, tracking, or developer-operated cloud service.

What the app saves

When you scan, the app uses the camera and LiDAR to save room geometry, reference photos, depth frames, camera poses, and tracking maps on your device. It also saves room names, scan times, device and system information, technical diagnostics, optional object-detail labels, and observations of printed reference cards. Photos and room geometry can reveal private details of your surroundings.

Permissions

Camera access is requested when you start a scan. Optional Mac pairing requests local-network access to discover and connect to House Control. You can revoke these permissions in Settings. Importing, reviewing, deleting, and sharing existing scans does not require camera access. The app does not request access to your Contacts, location, microphone, or Photos library.

Sharing and Files

When you use Share, iOS sends the exported ZIP to the app, service, device, or person you choose. Exports may contain reference photos, card observations and other recorded scan data, including technical diagnostics in session or whole-house exports. A room mesh can include nearby surfaces recorded during the same session. Review the destination before sharing. Copies you share or save elsewhere are controlled by that destination and are not removed when you delete the original.

Optional paired Mac capture

After you pair and connect to House Control on your Mac, the scanner sends bounded live geometry and coverage previews while scanning. Finished scan files, including photos and depth data, are transferred only when you select a saved capture to send. You can download the matching printed-card kit from that Mac. Transfers use an authenticated, encrypted local connection; its pairing key is stored in the device Keychain and is not included in scan exports or printed cards. This connection cannot operate home devices. Disconnect in Paired capture to stop communication. Originals stay on your iPhone, and copies received by the Mac must be managed separately.

Storage, backups and deletion

Scans are stored in the app’s Documents folder, also accessible through Files. Your device backup settings may include this data in an Apple device backup. The app itself does not synchronize scans to a developer server. Rooms and individual scan versions moved to Recently Deleted remain stored until you restore them or explicitly delete them permanently. Deleting a house permanently removes its local scan data. Device backups and exported copies must be managed separately.

Imported scans and temporary exports

When you import a folder, the app copies its room data into local storage. The original folder remains where you selected it. Temporary ZIP files are used to prepare shares and are removed after the share sheet closes; abandoned temporary shares are cleaned up later.

Changes and support

This policy describes this version of 3D Model Anything. Updates will include a revised policy if the app’s data practices change. Support contact information is provided on the app’s App Store support page once published.
